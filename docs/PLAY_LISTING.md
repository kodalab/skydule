## Full description (4000 max)
Skydule is a calendar for the events already on your phone, wrapped in a sky that changes with the time of day.

It reads and writes the calendars you already have, whichever account they sync from, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to move over. Your events stay where they are.

SEE YOUR DAY YOUR WAY
• Schedule: a scrolling list of what's coming, with today's date and weather up top
• Week: a time grid with your events laid out by the hour
• Month: the whole month at a glance, tap a day to jump into it

A SKY THAT MOVES WITH YOU
The background follows the time of day, from dawn through bright day to dusk and night. Prefer something plainer? Choose Dynamic sky (shaded by the current weather), Material You, or AMOLED black.

YOUR DATE ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Switch on the Dynamic app icon and the month and day appear right on the icon, over a sky that matches dawn, day, dusk or night. See the date without opening anything. Prefer a fixed icon? Keep the default.

REMINDERS THAT WORK
• Reminders arrive on time, with Snooze and Dismiss right in the notification
• Pick how long Snooze lasts
• Set a default alert for every new event

SMALL THINGS DONE WELL
• Weather beside today's date (optional, uses your approximate location)
• Choose Celsius or Fahrenheit, and which day your week starts on
• Add places to events with suggestions as you type
• No calendar on your phone yet? Create one that stays on this device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your events never leave your phone through Skydule
• Weather and place suggestions are the only things sent online, and only when you use them

Skydule needs access to your calendar to work, and notification permission for reminders. Location is optional and only used for weather.
