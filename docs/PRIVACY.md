# Skydule Privacy Policy

_Updated October 2026_

## Your data stays on your phone
Your calendar events, settings and reminders are stored only on your device. Skydule reads and writes the calendars
already on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we don't run any servers.

## What is sent online, only when you use it
- **Weather:** your approximate location is sent to a weather service (Open-Meteo) to get the forecast. It isn't stored.
- **Place suggestions:** text you type in an event's Location field is sent to a place-search service to suggest places.

## Permissions
- **Calendar:** to show, create, edit and delete your events.
- **Approximate location (optional):** for local weather.
- **Notifications and alarms:** to show reminders on time.
- **Internet:** for the weather and place suggestions above.

## Sharing and your choices
We never sell or share your data. You can turn off any permission at any time, and uninstalling Skydule removes
everything it stored. Your events stay in your calendar accounts.
