# Skydule Privacy Policy

_Updated October 2026_

## Your data stays on your phone
Your calendar events, settings and reminders are stored only on your device. Skydule reads and writes the calendars
already on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run any servers.

## What is sent online, only when you use it
- **Weather:** your approximate location is sent to a weather service (Open-Meteo) to get the forecast. It isn't stored.
- **Place suggestions:** text you type in an event's Location field, and your approximate location if you allowed it (to rank nearby places first), are sent to Google Places, or to OpenStreetMap's Photon service if Google can't be used, to suggest places. Nothing is stored by Skydule.
- **Crash reports (optional, on by default; switch off in Settings > About):** if Skydule crashes or hits an error, anonymous technical details go to Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it: the error, app version, phone model, Android version and a random installation ID. We don't add your events, calendars or places.

## Permissions
- **Calendar:** to show, create, edit and delete your events.
- **Approximate location (optional):** for local weather and nearby place suggestions.
- **Notifications and alarms:** to show reminders on time.
- **Internet:** for the weather, place suggestions and crash reports above.

## Sharing and your choices
We never sell or share your data. You can turn off any permission at any time, and uninstalling Skydule removes
everything it stored. Your events stay in your calendar accounts.
